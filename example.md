# How to Create a Strong Password

This guide explains how to create a strong password to keep 
your accounts safe.

## Why Strong Passwords Matter

Weak passwords are one of the most common reasons accounts 
get hacked. A strong Password makes it much harder for 
attackers to access your accounts.

## What Makes a Password Strong

A strong password should have the following:

- At least 12 characters
- A mix of uppercase and lowercase letters
- Numbers and special characters like ! or @
- No personal information like your name or birthday

## How to Create a Strong password

1. Think of a random phrase like "BlueSky$Rain42"
2. Mix uppercase and lowercase letters
3. Add numbers and special characters
4. Avoid using the same password for multiple accounts

## Using a Password Manager

A password-manager helps you store all your passwords safely. 
You only need to remember one master password. Popular 
Password Managers include:

- 1Password
- Bitwarden
- LastPass

## What to Avoid

Never use the following as passwords:

- Your name or birthday
- Simple sequences like "123456" or "password"
- The same password across multiple accounts

## Updating Your Passwords

You should update your passwords regularly. If a website tells 
you that your data was breached, change your password 
immediately and update the same password on other sites too.
