# Documentation Review Report

## Clarity

The text is generally clear and easy to understand for a general audience. However, there are a few areas that could be improved:

- The phrase "update the same password on other sites too" in the **Updating Your Passwords** section is potentially confusing. It likely means "change passwords on other sites where you used the same password," but the wording makes it sound like you should reuse the same password elsewhere.
- The example "BlueSky$Rain42" is described as a "random phrase," but it's actually a password example, not just a phrase. The instructions could be clearer about the creation method.
- The connection between the advice to "never use the same password across multiple accounts" and the benefit of password managers could be made more explicit.

## Missing Information

The following important information is missing:

- **How to remember strong passwords** without a password manager (memory techniques, passphrases, etc.)
- **How often** passwords should be updated regularly (e.g., every 3 months, 6 months, annually)
- **Two-factor authentication (2FA)** is not mentioned as an additional security layer
- **What to do if you forget your master password** for a password manager
- **How password managers work** and why they're secure (readers may be hesitant to trust them)
- **Password strength checkers** or tools to test password security
- **Specific examples** of what constitutes "personal information" beyond name and birthday (pet names, addresses, phone numbers, etc.)
- **How to securely share passwords** when necessary
- **What makes a good master password** for a password manager

## Inconsistent Terminology

The following terminology inconsistencies exist:

- **"Password"** vs **"password"** - Inconsistent capitalization throughout:
  - "strong Password" (capitalized mid-sentence in section 2)
  - "Password Managers" (capitalized in section 5)
  - "strong password" (lowercase in the title of section 4)
  - "master password" (lowercase in section 5)
  
- **"password-manager"** vs **"Password Manager"** vs **"Password Managers"** - Inconsistent hyphenation and capitalization:
  - "password-manager" (hyphenated, lowercase in first mention)
  - "Password Managers" (no hyphen, capitalized in the bullet list)

- **"accounts"** - Used consistently, which is good

**Recommendation:** Standardize "password" to lowercase except when part of a proper product name, and use "password manager" (two words, no hyphen, lowercase) consistently.
